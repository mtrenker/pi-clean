## Modifications

This repository renamed the skill to `prose-quality`, narrowed its scope to human-facing
prose, condensed its common rules, and moved its long-tail patterns into an optional reference.
It adds technical-accuracy, uncertainty, accessibility, project-vocabulary, and artifact-voice
guardrails. It omits advice to manufacture opinions, first-person experience, or deliberate
mess. The description carries a compact rule set for Pi's progressive disclosure.
